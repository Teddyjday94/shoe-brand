FOOT LABELLER

WHAT TO DO

  • Each foot has 8 dots. The model's guess is already there: drag any wrong dot to the right place.
  • Place dots where a shoe would be on this foot: socks and bare feet too.
  • Dot hidden behind the foot or a leg? Still put it where it is, then right-click it (hollow = hidden).
  • Dot outside the picture: drag it off the edge.
  • A box that isn't a foot: click it, DELETE FOOT. A foot with no dots: + ADD FOOT, click on it, then fix the dots.
  • Big toe side is inner. If inner/outer are the wrong way round, press SWAP SIDES.
  • Blurry, or can't tell where the foot is: UNUSABLE.

THE 8 DOTS

KEYS

  • Enter: save & next. A: previous. J: next undone.
  • Right-click a dot (or hover + H): hidden on/off.
  • Tab: select the other foot. Mouse wheel: zoom, drag empty space: pan, Z: reset view.
