[ QUESTIONS PEOPLE KEEP ASKING ]

WHY ONLY ONE SHOE?
Because the second one would be worse. We would rather make the first one again, slightly better, four hundred times.
IS IT FOR RUNNING?
It is for walking to the place where other people are running. It will survive the walk back.
WHY DOES IT SQUEAK?
See rule 03. The squeak goes away around week six. We miss it when it does.
WHERE IS IT MADE?
In a building with a loading dock, labelled B. You can watch it on the LAB page.
CAN I BUY ONE?
No. This is a mock-up. But you can reserve one in the SHOP and feel something.
